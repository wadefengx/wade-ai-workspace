# Business Knowledge

## Purpose

Store durable business context that explains why the product exists and what success looks like.

## Include

- Problem statements, target users, and value hypotheses.
- Business constraints that change prioritization or scope.
- Definitions of success metrics that matter across phases.

## Exclude

- Implementation notes that belong in specs or architecture docs.
- Temporary meeting notes without durable value.

## Curation rules

1. Prefer concise reference over narrative history.
2. Update when product direction changes, not every implementation detail.
3. Link to specs that realize a business goal instead of duplicating them.
