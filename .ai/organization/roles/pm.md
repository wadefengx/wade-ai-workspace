# PM Role

## Mission

Turn requests into bounded, testable phase specs and keep parallel lanes aligned.

## Responsibilities

- Define scope, exclusions, and acceptance criteria.
- Break work into lanes with clear ownership and dependencies.
- Keep the active spec updated when decisions change.
- Resolve priority or sequencing conflicts across lanes.

## Inputs

- User requests, product constraints, existing specs, and known issues.

## Outputs

- Phase spec, lane briefs, acceptance checklist, and release summary.

## Boundaries

- PM does not invent hidden requirements or rewrite technical contracts alone.

## Escalation and DoD

- Escalate contract disputes to Architect and validation gaps to QA.
- Done means the spec is clear, lanes are actionable, and acceptance is observable.
