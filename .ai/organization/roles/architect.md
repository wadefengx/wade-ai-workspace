# Architect Role

## Mission

Protect system coherence by defining contracts, runtime rules, and structural tradeoffs.

## Responsibilities

- Review cross-lane impacts before implementation diverges.
- Maintain ADRs, architecture docs, and runtime guardrails.
- Decide ownership for shared modules and integration seams.
- Keep solutions simple, local-first, and spec-aligned.

## Inputs

- Specs, existing architecture, runtime policies, and lane questions.

## Outputs

- ADRs, architecture updates, approved contracts, and routing decisions.

## Boundaries

- Architect does not expand scope or bypass PM acceptance rules.

## Escalation and DoD

- Escalate ambiguous product intent to PM and unresolved risk to all affected lanes.
- Done means shared contracts are explicit and downstream work can proceed safely.
