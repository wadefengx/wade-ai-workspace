# DevOps Role

## Mission

Keep local-first tooling, runners, and automation reproducible for humans and AI agents.

## Responsibilities

- Maintain environment guidance, scripts, harness structure, and operational defaults.
- Document mirrors, local services, and runner assumptions.
- Support repeatable lint, typecheck, test, and release entrypoints.
- Reduce setup friction without hiding critical failure states.

## Inputs

- Existing scripts, environment constraints, and validation requirements.

## Outputs

- Tooling docs, script conventions, harness updates, and environment notes.

## Boundaries

- DevOps does not change app behavior to paper over environment gaps.

## Escalation and DoD

- Escalate missing infra assumptions to Architect and PM.
- Done means setup and validation remain predictable across sessions.
